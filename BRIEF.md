### What Google reviewers mention (Google's own topic summary)
- Positive: **Atmosphere 96%** (26 mentions), **Family 100%** (9), **Nature 100%** (5), **Service 87%** (105), **Property 87%** (77), **Breakfast 86%** (21), **Business 86%** (14), **Location 83%** (36), **Dining 81%** (47).
- Weak spots: Sleep (80% unfavourable, 5 mentions), Fitness (29% unfavourable), Room (27%), Pool (22%), Cleanliness (17%).
- Google's summary: guests praise comfortable rooms, great food, friendly staff and a peaceful atmosphere. They complain about flies in the dining area, old mattresses, room upkeep and disorganised parking.

### What Tripadvisor reviewers say (paraphrased, 2018–2019)
- Love: courteous, helpful staff; tasty food and a cheap, varied buffet breakfast (aloo paratha, uttapam, puri-bhaji, upma, omelette); a garden and a "super swimming pool"; "tucked away from the hustle of Lonavala market"; the 3–4 bedroom **bungalows** suit big groups; good for business-cum-leisure trips.
- Complain: linen not changed daily; a damp or cramped room; the restaurant is on a lower level with no lift (hard for elderly guests); no railing at the pool steps; you need a car (about 4–5 km from the station by one reviewer's count).

**Rule:** the site quotes no individual review. It uses only the Google rating, the count and Google's topic percentages.

## Location and nearby

- In Raiwood (Ryewood), Thombrewadi, about 1.5 km from Lonavala centre (Agoda) and "tucked away from the market" (Tripadvisor review).
- **Ryewood Park: about an 18-minute walk** (Hotels.com). Agoda gives about 1 km.
- **Wet N Joy Water Park** and **Della Adventure**: within a 15-minute drive ([almosafer/Expedia copy](https://global.almosafer.com/en/hotel/details/atg/oyo-7293-basilica-holiday-home-2104166), [flyin](https://www.flyin.com/en/property/india/lonavala/basilica-holiday-home-4066025.ar.html)).
- Celebrity Wax Museum: on Agoda's list of nearby attractions. Drive time not confirmed, so the site says "short drive".
- Shingroba Temple about 1.1 km (0.7 mi) and Narayani Dham Temple about 2.1 km (1.3 mi) (Tripadvisor).
- Lonavala railway station: a taxi ride away. Agoda gives 1.5 km to the city centre, and one reviewer says 4–5 km.
- From Mumbai about 83 km and from Pune about 65 km via the Mumbai–Pune Expressway (general road distances). **Shown as approximate.**
- Other nearby places named on Agoda: Bhushi Dam, Lion's Point, Tiger's Leap, Karla Caves, Lonavala Lake.
