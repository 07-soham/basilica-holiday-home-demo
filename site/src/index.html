Why book with the house, not the app

  • No app commission on your booking. OYO, MakeMyTrip, Goibibo and Expedia each take a cut. Booking here sends it all to the property.
  • Talk to the front desk, not a call centre. Ask for a garden view, a balcony, or rooms next to each other on WhatsApp.
  • Direct-booking offer: owner to add (for example, free breakfast or a set discount)

Rooms, and whole bungalows for the group

Every room has AC, free Wi-Fi, a 32-inch TV, a pillow menu, bottled water and daily housekeeping. Bring the whole family: the 4-bedroom bungalow sleeps 15.

    {{ROOMS}}

Also available: a room with an extra bed (sleeps 3) and a 2-bedroom with a garden (sleeps 6). Extra beds are ₹700 a night.

{{IMG:pool|The oval swimming pool with a red-tiled deck and white loungers, against a steep wooded slope|100vw|lazy}}

A pool below the trees

Outdoor pool with cabanas, open 8 am to 6 pm. Swim in the morning, dry off on the terrace, and be at Ryewood Park on foot in under 20 minutes.

Breakfast before the hills

The in-house restaurant serves a buffet breakfast and dinner, and room service runs through the day. Guests mention aloo paratha, uttapam, puri bhaji and upma at breakfast.

Owner to add: restaurant name, timings and a short veg / non-veg menu
